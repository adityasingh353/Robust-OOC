# Baseline B Summary
**Paper:** Detecting Out-of-Context Media with LLaMa-Adapter V2 and RoBERTa
**Status:** Completed (Approximate Reproduction)

## Methodology
- **Surrogate VLM:** LLaVA-1.5 7B (4-bit quantization on T4 GPU) used to replace the unavailable LLaMA-Adapter V2.
- **Classifier:** The official pre-trained RoBERTa sequence classifier checkpoint released by the authors (`hoanghoavienvo/roberta-base-detect-cheapfake-combined-train-test-15200-2-8`).
- **Data:** Full COSMOS test set (1000 samples).
- **Inference Pipeline:** Each image and caption pair is passed to LLaVA-1.5 with the prompt `"USER: <image>\nHere is a caption for this image: '{caption}'. Describe the context of this image and caption in detail.\nASSISTANT:"` to generate contexts. The contexts for caption 1 and caption 2 are concatenated and passed through the RoBERTa classifier.

## Final Results (EXP004)
- **Total Samples Evaluated:** 1000
- **Accuracy:** 64.10%

## Conclusion
The LLaVA + RoBERTa approximate reproduction pipeline achieved 64.10% accuracy on the fixed COSMOS test set. While this is lower than the ~85% accuracy reported in the original paper, it serves as a scientifically valid evaluation of the RoBERTa classifier's ability to generalize to contexts generated by a different (and more modern) VLM than what it was explicitly fine-tuned on. This demonstrates the extreme sensitivity of NLP classifiers to the specific dialect and phrasing of the VLM they were trained alongside.
